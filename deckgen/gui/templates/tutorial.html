User Guide — DeckGen Studio

End-to-end workflow from project setup to print-ready PDF sheet export.

1. Projects, Root Directory & state.json

  • On Home, click Create Project to link a new or existing Root Directory.
  • DeckGen automatically initializes state.json along with cards/fronts, cards/backs, cards/json, and sheets/.
  • Use Load Project (or the Saved Projects list) to reopen a folder or load a saved state.json. Only the active project is loaded into memory.

2. Card Builder: Importing, Global Variants & Loaded vs. Ready Lists

  • Import cards: Upload an MTGO .txt decklist, single .png files, or an entire .png folder, or search by card name (HD local JSONs first with Scryfall fallback).
  • Workflow lists: Imported cards appear in Loaded Cards. Check Ready on finished cards to move them to Ready to Export (uncheck anytime to keep editing).
  • Safe editing: Click any card to open the detail editor and click ← Back to return to the lists with all edits saved to state.json.
  • Global variants: Pick a frame style and click Apply to entire library to update all loaded cards at once.

3. Template Crop Masks, Layers & Adjustments

  • Change Art: Opens a tabbed modal for HD JSONs, Scryfall, or Upload Image with visual previews.
  • Template Crop Masks: In ✂ Crop Art, choose Classic Art Box, Extended Art (Angled), Circular / Oval Vignette, or Full Art / Saga. Zoom and pan; the output preserves mask geometry and alpha.
  • Independent Layer Manager: Drag any layer (crown, pinlines, art, etc.) independently; a blue drop indicator shows where it will land.
  • Brightness & Background: Adjust artwork Brightness, customize the Background Color, or add translucent background boxes for full-art cards.

4. Deck Generator: Ribbon UI, Individual Scaling & Background Conversion

  • Unified Ribbon Bar: Configure source folder, paper (A3/A4/A4-3), orientation, cut marks, and view mode (Both, Only Fronts, or Only Backs) from one toolbar.
  • Dual Preview & Individual Scale: Page through Fronts and Backs independently. Click any card slot on the sheet to adjust its individual scale.
  • Automatic background conversion: Selecting a folder with .png/.jpg images automatically converts them to PDF in Python in the background.
  • Collision-safe export: Click Export Sheet and pick your DPI. If the destination exists, choose to Overwrite or create a sequential version (-1, -2, etc.).
